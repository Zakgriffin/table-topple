CCL steps
Hysteresis: any pixel above rho low can form growth edges, but a finished component is discarded unless one of its members clears rho high. CCL steps is a pure scrubber -- "auto" runs to the fixpoint (the real algorithm); 1+ caps the hook/compress rounds so components can be watched coalescing. It cannot change the converged result.
min region size
Min region size drops components below this many pixels before they ever become regions. 2 is free -- a 1-pixel component has no axis to fit, so it could only ever be fitted and discarded (measured: 1149 of 2931 regions). Above 2 it becomes a real evidence floor and does change output.
